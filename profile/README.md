## BuhoGO

The friendly Bitcoin wallet for Android and the web. Hold your own keys, pay anyone, spend anywhere.

- **Your keys.** Self-custodial Spark and Arkade wallets, or connect the wallet you already use through NWC or LNbits.
- **Pay anyone.** Lightning invoices and addresses, LNURL, Bitcoin addresses, Nostr profiles, and phone numbers in Kenya and Zambia.
- **Run a shop.** Kiosk mode turns a phone into a point of sale, with tips and Bolt Card payments.
- **No sign-up.** No account, no email, no ID. Open source under AGPL-3.0.

[Open in the browser](https://go.mybuho.de) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=mybuho.buhogo) · [Zapstore](https://zapstore.dev/apps/mybuho.buhogo) · [Docs](https://docs-buhogo.netlify.app) · [Source](https://github.com/Buho-Ecosystem/Buho_go)
